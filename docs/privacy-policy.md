# Sift Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: Draft

This draft is not legal advice. Before public launch, review it with a qualified lawyer and update placeholders such as legal entity, jurisdiction, and launch date.

## Overview

Sift is an allergy menu scanner that helps users review visible restaurant menu text for possible allergen risks. Sift is designed to provide practical risk prompts and staff questions. It does not guarantee food safety and is not a medical device.

## Information We Collect

Sift may collect or store the following information:

- Allergy profiles you create, including allergen names, severity, cross-contact preference, custom details, preferred app language, and restaurant language.
- Menu photos you choose to scan or upload.
- Scan results, including dish names, risk levels, explanations, questions to ask staff, and image readability status.
- Account information if login is enabled, such as Apple Sign In user identifier, email address, and display name when provided by Apple.
- Basic technical information needed to operate the app and analysis service, such as request timing, error logs, and service diagnostics.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and save allergy profiles on your device.
- Analyze selected menu images against your allergy profile.
- Show risk cards, explanations, and staff questions.
- Save scan history locally when enabled.
- Provide account access if login is enabled.
- Improve reliability, safety wording, and app performance.

## Local Storage

The MVP is local-first. Allergy profiles, onboarding state, restaurant language preferences, and scan history are stored on your device using local app storage unless cloud features are enabled later.

## AI Analysis

When live AI analysis is enabled, Sift sends the selected menu image, allergy profile, and restaurant language preference to Sift's server-side analysis function. That service may forward the request to an AI provider to produce structured menu risk results.

Do not upload menus or profile details you do not want processed for analysis. Sift should not be used as a substitute for confirming ingredients and preparation with restaurant staff.

## AI Disclosure And Safety Limits

Sift uses automated analysis, including AI, to review selected menu photos, visible menu text, allergy profile details, and language preferences. AI analysis may be incomplete, inaccurate, or affected by unclear images, missing ingredients, translation issues, menu changes, or restaurant preparation methods that are not visible in the menu.

Sift does not guarantee that any food, dish, ingredient, restaurant, or preparation method is safe. Always confirm ingredients, preparation, and cross-contact risks with restaurant staff before ordering, especially for severe allergies.

## AI And Photo Processing

When you choose to scan or upload a menu image, Sift may send the selected image, visible menu text extracted from the image, allergy profile details, language preferences, and basic request metadata to Sift's analysis service. Sift's analysis service may send this information to an AI provider to generate structured menu risk results, explanations, readability status, and questions to ask restaurant staff.

Sift uses this information to provide the scan result you requested, operate the service, debug errors, prevent abuse, and improve reliability and safety wording. Sift does not use menu photos for advertising tracking.

Provider-specific processing, storage, and deletion terms should be added before launch once the final AI provider, hosting provider, analytics provider, crash reporting provider, and authentication provider are confirmed.

## Photos And Camera

Sift requests camera access only when you choose to scan a menu. Sift requests photo library access only when you choose to upload a menu image. Sift does not access photos for unrelated purposes.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate Sift, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or AI analysis providers. These providers should only process information for Sift's app functionality and service operation.

## App Store Privacy Summary

For App Store privacy disclosures, Sift may collect or process the following categories depending on enabled features:

- Allergy profile data: allergen names, severity, cross-contact preferences, custom notes, and language preferences. Used for app functionality and menu analysis. This may be sensitive or health-related user-provided information.
- Menu photos and scan content: selected menu images, visible menu text, dish names, scan results, explanations, and staff questions. Used for app functionality and AI menu analysis.
- Account identifiers: Apple Sign In user identifier, email address, and display name if account login is enabled and Apple provides them. Used for authentication and account functionality.
- Diagnostics: crash logs, error logs, request timing, and service diagnostics. Used for reliability, security, debugging, and service operation.
- Usage data: limited product interaction data may be used to understand app performance and improve core flows if analytics are enabled.

Sift does not sell personal information and does not use menu photos or allergy profile data for advertising tracking. App Store Connect privacy answers should be updated before submission to match the final implementation, third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, hosting, and AI providers.

## Retention And Deletion

Sift is local-first for the MVP. Allergy profiles, onboarding state, restaurant language preferences, and local scan history remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear history, uninstall the app, or use account deletion tools if cloud features are enabled.

When AI analysis is enabled, menu images, allergy profile details, language preferences, and request metadata may be processed by Sift's analysis service and AI provider to complete the scan. Sift should retain full menu images and AI request payloads only as long as needed to provide the scan, debug failures, prevent abuse, or meet legal and security obligations.

Server logs may include request timing, error details, service diagnostics, IP address, device or account identifiers, and similar technical data. These logs should be retained for no longer than the period set in Sift's production retention policy unless needed for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or service reliability.

If account login or cloud sync is enabled, account data is retained until you delete your account or request deletion, unless Sift must retain limited records for legal, security, billing, or abuse-prevention reasons.

To request deletion or ask a privacy question, contact support@siftallergy.app.

## Children's Privacy And Family Profiles

Sift may be used by parents or guardians to manage allergy profiles for family members, including children. Sift is not intended for unsupervised use by children under the age required by applicable law, and children should not create accounts or submit information without a parent or guardian.

Parents and guardians are responsible for the allergy profiles they create, the information they provide, and decisions made using Sift results. Family or child profiles may include allergy-related information, profile names or nicknames, severity settings, notes, language preferences, menu photos, and scan results.

Before launching family accounts, child profiles, cloud sync, or features directed to children, Sift should review COPPA, age-gating, parental consent, account deletion, and sensitive-data handling requirements with qualified counsel.

## Safety Notice

Sift analyzes visible menu text and common ingredient patterns. It cannot confirm ingredients, kitchen preparation, or cross-contact. Always confirm with restaurant staff, especially for severe allergies.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Edit or delete allergy profiles in the app.
- Clear local scan history.
- Decline camera or photo access and avoid using scan/upload features.
- Sign out if account login is enabled.
- Contact support about privacy questions.
