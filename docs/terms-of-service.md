## 1. Overview

Sift is an allergy menu scanner that helps users identify possible allergen risks from visible restaurant menu text and common ingredient patterns. Sift provides practical prompts, explanations, and questions to ask restaurant staff.

## 2. No Safety Guarantee

Sift cannot guarantee that any food, dish, ingredient, restaurant, or preparation method is safe. Sift cannot see the kitchen, verify ingredients, inspect cross-contact, or replace staff confirmation.

You are responsible for confirming ingredients, preparation, and cross-contact risks with restaurant staff before ordering, especially for severe allergies.

## 3. Not Medical Advice

Sift is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical questions, allergy diagnosis, emergency planning, or treatment decisions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## 4. User Responsibilities

You agree to:

- Provide accurate allergy profile information.
- Review Sift results carefully and conservatively.
- Confirm uncertain or high-risk items with restaurant staff.
- Avoid relying on Sift as your only source of food safety information.
- Use Sift lawfully and responsibly.

## 5. AI And Translation Limitations

Sift may use AI systems to analyze menu images and generate staff questions or translated messages. AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, or affected by unclear images, missing menu details, language issues, or hidden restaurant preparation methods.

Translations are provided for convenience and may not be perfect. When possible, confirm important allergy statements with a fluent speaker or restaurant staff.
